# Team block photos

Example photos for the Team blocks on the docs site and in Storybook. The blocks ship none; each person takes a `photo` prop.

All photos are from [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com/?utm_source=fasla-ui&utm_medium=referral) under the [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license). Free photos only, no Unsplash+. They are the photos the Figma Team set shows, traced to their Unsplash page by image search and confirmed against the Figma image (a mean pixel difference under 5 of 255; a different photo scores above 25).

`portrait-*.webp` are the originals at 800 × 1000. `lineup-*.webp` and `roster-*.webp` are **transparent cut-outs** of the same photos (background removed locally with macOS Vision, then cropped to the figure for the Lineup and to the tile for the Roster). They are derivative works of the photos below, used under the same licence.
