# Contact Us photo credits

The photos are from [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com/?utm_source=fasla-ui&utm_medium=referral) under the [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license): free to use, commercially too, with no permission or attribution needed. Free photos only, no Unsplash+. Each photo's page was opened on 2026-10-31 and says "Free to use under the Unsplash License". Each download was recorded with Unsplash when the photo was added.

The first four are the photos Figma's Contact Us frames show. Amman is the closest free match to Figma's view of the citadel, not the same frame. The last two were found by a reverse image search on the originals exported from Figma, and each Unsplash photo matches its Figma original to within JPEG noise (the same picture, the same 3:2 shape).

Each file is the photo with nothing cropped off, resized to a 1600px long edge (2400px for Split Photo Dark and Photo & Dark Panel, whose slots crop a landscape photo to a near-square box) and saved as WebP at quality 80. The block crops it to each size's image slot and, where Figma crops off the middle, a `focus` in `apps/docs/components/blocks/contact-us-photos.ts` says where. Nothing else was changed: no colour grading, retouching, blur or masking. Whatever the photos show, such as the laptop's logo and the slides in the hall, is as Figma shows it.
